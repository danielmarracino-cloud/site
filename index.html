The Creative Core

Live effects, freely combined.

Select any objects and stack effects in real time — nothing rebuilds, nothing blocks. A spinning, rainbow-trailed, exploded dodecahedron is six taps away.

↻ Motion

Per-object spin and whole-group spin about the centroid. Time is the only input — drag a speed slider and it lands on the very next frame.

◑ Color Spread

Every object is recolored along an HSV gradient by index — or cycle the whole spectrum on the golden angle for an animated rainbow sweep.

✺ Explode

Each face slides out along its own normal. The slider is piecewise — gentle in the middle, dramatic at the extremes — for both fine control and full detonation.

◎ Auras & Shells

Wrap any solid in concentric wireframe shells — your shape or another, one to eight deep, with spacing, thickness, fade and glow. The signature Athena halo.

✧ Trails

Particle systems trail every object as it travels — laser beams, plasma, ribbons, embers, confetti. Make them rainbow and let zoom drive the warp.

▦ Groups & Layers

Organize objects into independent groups, each with its own shape, count and effects. Spin one cluster while another breathes on a travelling wave.
