the idea

A studio for solids, on a canvas of light.

Athena turns pure geometry into a living scene. Tap to summon, drag to arrange, and let effects run while you compose.

47 solids, one tap

From the five Platonic solids to Johnson, Catalan and Goldberg — every shape derived from clean geometry.

Effects that move

Spin, colour-spread, explode, wireframe auras and particle trails — all live, all at once.

Capture in motion

Shoot a photo or record video straight from the scene, then save and share.

the catalog

Six families. Every one earns its place.

Each solid is built from first principles using the same geometric operators, so the whole catalog stays consistent — and correct.

Platonic 5 solids

The originals — perfectly regular, the foundation everything else is derived from.

Archimedean truncate · ambo

Built by truncating and rectifying the Platonics — the soccer-ball and friends.

Catalan the duals

Each is the dual of an Archimedean — flip faces for vertices and back again.

Kepler–Poinsot star forms

Stellated and spiked — the regular star polyhedra, rendered as faithful art.

Goldberg chamfer

Geodesic-like spheres of hexagons and pentagons, grown by chamfering.

Johnson 92 forms

Pyramids, cupolas and bipyramids — composed procedurally, piece by piece.
