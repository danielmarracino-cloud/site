The catalog
Five families. Forty-four solids.

Every solid is derived from first principles in code — no mesh files, no imported models. Tap one and Athena builds it live.

5
Platonic
The five regular convex solids. Identical regular faces, identical vertices.
11
Archimedean
Semi-regular solids of two or more regular polygons — 11 of the 13 ship today.
11
Catalan
Duals of the Archimedeans — face-transitive, beautifully irregular.
4
Kepler–Poinsot
The four regular star polyhedra. Self-intersecting, luminous, strange.
13
Johnson
A hand-composed procedural subset — 13 of the 92 strictly-convex Johnson solids.

The studio
Wrap it in light and motion

Colour, spin, shells, auras, trails and travel paths — layered live on the solid, the way a brush works in Procreate.

14 colour harmonies

The real ColorHarmony system — Rainbow, Golden, Complementary, Triadic, Neon, Earth, Ice and more, each with its own hue math. Default base hue 0.55 (teal).

Spin on any axis

Tumble, or lock spin to X, Y or Z. Add group spin and a two-finger tilt for hands-on rotation.

Shells · OBB

Nested glowing cages that follow the solid's shape. Tune count, spacing, glow, breathe, pulse, rotate and rainbow.

Auras

Soft radial blooms that breathe around the solid, tinted by the active harmony — the glow that gives a scene its mood.

30+ trails

Comet, fire, plasma, lightning, chromatic and beyond — motion that paints a path behind every moving object.

Size variation

Scale a group by Fibonacci, Euler, Kepler or duality — mathematical rhythm across copies of a solid.

Travel paths

Send objects sliding like beads on a wire — orbits and routes that animate the whole composition.

Game of Life

Conway's cellular automaton, turned into a creative tool — living patterns that drive the scene.

Light & quality

Glow, bloom and render quality controls — dial the scene from soft ambient to crisp and bright.

Geometry, grown from code

No 3D files ship in Athena. Each solid is computed from shared vertex math — which means it's exact, light, and endlessly recolourable.

01
Seed vertices

Start from a family's defining coordinates — golden-ratio points, symmetry groups, dual pairs.

02
Build faces

Connect vertices into faces and derive edges; Archimedeans truncate, Catalans dualize.

03
Compose Johnson set

The 13-solid Johnson subset is hand-assembled from primitive caps and prisms.

04
Light it live

SceneKit shades the mesh; ColorHarmony tints faces; shells & auras layer on top.
